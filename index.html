Curriculum Vitae

Cemal Melih Tanış is working as a research engineer in the group of Satellite Services and Research of Arctic Space Centre of Finnish Meteorological Institute. He has completed his master’s programme in Aalto University in 2019 and he has a scientific and engineering background in electronics, nanotechnology and space technology. He has over 8 years of professional experience in remote sensing of snow and vegetation, research, product development, maintenance and validation, software development, web development, user uptake and networking activities, in different national and international projects and actions. He is interested in innovative solutions and futuristic concepts not only in his own expertise but in every field of science and every part of life.
